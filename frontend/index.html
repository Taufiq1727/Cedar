Critical Gaps in Current Workflow

  • ✕ No time to review old prescriptions, lab reports, or discharge summaries
  • ✕ Incomplete history leads to missed diagnoses and duplicate tests
  • ✕ Patients struggle to articulate symptoms in clinical terminology
  • ✕ Language barriers in multilingual regions affect care quality
  • ✕ Paper records are unstructured and often illegible
How It Works

From Patient Check-In to Doctor-Ready Summary

MediKiosk transforms the pre-consultation workflow into a structured, AI-powered process.

1

Patient Check-In

Patient registers, selects language, gives consent, and begins the AI-guided intake.

2

AI Clinical Intake

Conversational questions adapt to the patient's condition. Voice or text input in 3 languages.

3

Document Processing

Upload prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries. OCR + AI extracts key clinical data.

4

Doctor Reviews

Key Features

Built for Indian Healthcare

Every feature is designed to solve real problems in high-volume hospital OPDs.

🧠

AI Clinical Intake

Adaptive questioning engine with 5 clinical pathways. Gemini AI extracts structured information from natural language.

🎤

Multilingual Voice Input

Speak in English, Hindi, or Kannada. Built-in speech recognition with text editing and confirmation.

📄

Document OCR + AI Extraction

Upload prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries. AI extracts diagnoses, medications, and lab values.

🚨

Red Flag Detection

Rule-based engine detects priority symptoms in real-time. No LLM dependency for safety-critical alerts.

📋

AI Clinical Summary

Generates physician-ready HPI, PMH, medications, allergies, and assessment. Editable and approvable by doctors.

📊

Medical Timeline

Chronological view of diagnoses, medications, procedures, and consultations. Auto-generated from all patient data.
